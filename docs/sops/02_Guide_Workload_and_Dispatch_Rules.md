# TokioTours — Guide Workload & Dispatch Rules

Upload to Shared Drive SOPs as `02_Guide_Workload_and_Dispatch_Rules`.

## Tour hours vs extras
- City Tour catalog prices already include the guided experience — **do not** add a separate private-guide hourly invoice line
- Ticket / place extras (TeamLab, admissions) **do not** consume guide hours

## Night tour exemption
- Short **~3-hour night tour** blocks are treated as workload exemptions relative to a full daytime day
- Do not stack a full heavy daytime day + night block without Ops review

## Matching
- Dispatch by language preference, family/party needs, and city focus
- Use GUID channel for guide coordination; Concierge retains master CUST visibility
