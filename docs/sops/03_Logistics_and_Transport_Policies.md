# TokioTours — Logistics & Transport Policies

Upload to Shared Drive SOPs as `03_Logistics_and_Transport_Policies`.

## Suica / PASMO / IC
- When guest chooses subway / IC transit, record Suica needed + value (€)
- Ticket procurement and vouchers route through **TIX** (Ticketer)
- Guest chat can share seating maps / QR images when Ticketer Direct Chat is ON

## Private chauffeur
- Alphard / HiAce capacity must fit party size
- Driver coordination uses **DRIV** channel
- Driver Direct Chat may be toggled by Concierge/Ops for guest route coordination

## Specialist Direct Chat
- Temporary privilege: Ticketer / Driver direct guest messaging ON/OFF from Comms Hub
- Guest sees branded TokioTours specialist titles; Ops sees role-color badges
- Vouchers and chat image attachments purge ~2 days after tour end
