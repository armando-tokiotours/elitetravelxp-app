# TokioTours — Pricing & Discount Rules

Upload this into Shared Drive: **TokioTours - Operations & Concierge SOPs** as `01_Pricing_and_Discount_Rules`.

## Concierge deposit
- Default commitment fee: **€60**
- When paid: **100% credited** to the guest tour balance
- Status path: `UNPAID` → `FEE_PAID` → `PARTIALLY_PAID` (30% progress) → `FULLY_PAID`

## Agent discount authority
- Concierge agents may discount **billable** cart lines by up to **15%** without Ops Manager approval
- Reductions **> 15%** of cart billable base require Owner / Ops Manager override
- Complimentary bonuses (€0 / FREE BONUS) are excluded from the 15% calculation

## Progress payments
- 30% progress milestone ≈ deposit + 30% of (package base − fee)
- Vendor dispatch guidance unlocks after the 30% milestone; guest-facing full confirmations follow `FULLY_PAID`
